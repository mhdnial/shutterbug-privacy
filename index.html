Shutterbug — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Shutterbug is a wildlife photography game for Android, made by the Shutterbug student team for the UMPSA x HUAWEI GameCenter Mobile App Competition 2026. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Shutterbug app published on HUAWEI AppGallery.

Summary

  • You can play without signing in (guest play).
  • If you sign in with your HUAWEI ID, the game uses your display name, your profile picture and an ID number for this app (the OpenID) only while it is running. The game does not store them or send them to us.
  • Your progress, settings and in-game photos are saved only on your phone.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking, and we run no servers.

1. Information the game uses

a. HUAWEI ID sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in, HUAWEI Account Kit (part of HUAWEI Mobile Services, provided by Huawei) gives the game:

InformationWhy the game uses it
Your HUAWEI ID display nameTo show "Signed in as …" on the home and settings screens
Your HUAWEI ID profile pictureTo show it next to your name; it is downloaded to show and never saved
Your OpenID, a code that identifies your HUAWEI ID for this app onlyTo know that you are signed in

The game keeps these only in the phone's memory while it runs. It does not write them to storage or send them to the Shutterbug team or anyone else. We do not receive your phone number, email address, contacts or location.

Huawei processes the sign-in itself under its own terms and privacy statement for HUAWEI ID and HUAWEI Mobile Services.

b. Game data saved on your phone

The game saves the following in its own storage on your phone:

  • your progress: reputation, level scores and the Field Journal
  • your settings: volumes, camera mode and similar choices
  • small thumbnails of the best in-game photo of each animal

These are pictures of the game's own scenes. The game does not use your phone's camera or your photo gallery. This data never leaves your phone.

c. Permissions

The game asks Android for network access: internet, network state and Wi-Fi state. These are needed for HUAWEI ID sign-in. The game asks for no other permissions: no camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information.
  • We do not show advertising or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not offer purchases or collect payment details.

3. Children

Shutterbug is made for children aged about 8 to 12 and their families.

  • The game can be fully played as a guest, without signing in.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • A HUAWEI ID is only used for sign-in as described above.

Parents and guardians can choose guest play, and can remove all game data at any time (see section 4).

4. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Sign out: Settings → HUAWEI ID → Sign out.
  • Delete progress: Settings → Reset progress. This erases level scores, the Field Journal, photos and achievements on this phone.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, which removes all its data from the phone.
  • Remove the game's access to your HUAWEI ID: use the account settings of your HUAWEI ID on your phone (for example, the list of apps signed in with it).

Because we keep no data on our own servers, there is nothing further for us to delete. You can still contact us with any question.

5. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Sign-in is handled by Huawei's own secure service.

6. Changes to this policy

If the game changes what information it uses (for example, a future cloud save tied to your HUAWEI ID), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released. The new policy will also be linked in the app listing.
